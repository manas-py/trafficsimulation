Model & Cycle Summary

Cycle control now matches the prescribed six-step plan exactly: 10 s of north/south through service (d1, d2, u2, u3), 5 s of protected north/south turns (d3, u1), 10 s of east/west through service (r1, r2, l2, l3), and 5 s of protected east/west turns (r3, l1). The sequence then repeats with no overlap between conflicting streams. Poisson arrivals from Sec. 4.1 are sampled once per approach and randomly assigned to the lane-specific turn options according to their prevalence, reproducing the stochastic platoons documented in the manuscript.

The canvas draws the 12-lane geometry exactly as prescribed: three approach lanes per leg with explicit mapping to the downstream lanes (wr, er, wl, el, nu, su, nd, sd), textured asphalt, crosswalks, and stylized vehicles so the visuals match the reference figure while still highlighting signal permissions and queue growth governed by the microscopic driver-delay physics.
